How It All Started

I couldn't find what
I was looking for.
So I made it myself.

Hi, I'm Shir! I'm 12 years old and I'm honestly obsessed with balloons. It started when I was planning my own birthday party and I really wanted one of those big beautiful balloon arches I kept seeing online...

I searched everywhere in the Algarve. Nothing felt right. Either the style wasn't what I imagined, or it was way too expensive for what it was. So I thought... okay, I'll just try to do it myself. How hard can it be?

"It took me a whole afternoon, I watched like a hundred videos, and honestly? I fell completely in love with it. Everyone at my party kept asking who did the balloons. That's when I realised — this could actually be a real thing."
— Shir

Now I make balloon decorations for other people's celebrations too! Every single arrangement is made by me, by hand, designed around what YOU want. I take it really seriously because I know how much your event matters to you.
